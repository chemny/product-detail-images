# Conditional Commerce Modules

Use these modules when they add purchase-decision value. Do not burden ordinary creative pages with their risk logic.

## Objective Modules

Reviews, exact parameters, certifications, awards, performance data, comparative results, brand history, and endorsements require visible, supplied, or otherwise confirmed information.

When selected information is missing, explain the status once in the consolidated plan and offer concise options: supply information, use a clearly identified design proposal, replace the module, or remove it. The user decides.

## Creative Material And Function Expression

Separate three expression levels:

- **Objective:** exact material, mechanism, benefit, or data; requires support.
- **Experiential:** describes a visible or ordinary sensory impression without precise performance data.
- **Conceptual:** uses color, light, water, air, botanical, geometric, cultural, or emotional metaphor without asserting measurable performance.

Experiential and conceptual expression may be created freely when it does not masquerade as a verified fact.

## Human Close-Up

Use when the body relationship genuinely explains fit, scale, neckline, drape, grip, wear, or use. If the generation system refuses or materially changes the selected framing, report the page and let the user choose a wider crop, product-only crop, mannequin, flat lay, or another module.

## CTA

- Off for product detail-page sections.
- Available only for an explicitly separate product header, campaign asset, landing hero, or conversion page.
- Treat CTA as a project-level choice, never a mandatory per-page element.

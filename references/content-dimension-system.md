# Content Dimension System

Start from the complete commerce story and stable skeleton in `poster-sequence.md`, then choose the strongest supported expression for each slot.

## Value Tree

Before selecting pages, define:

- one core value: the primary buyer outcome or reason to care;
- 1-3 advantage pillars that make the value believable;
- visible or supplied evidence for each pillar;
- usage imagination;
- purchase-decision information and likely buyer concerns.

The page set must express the tree as a coherent narrative rather than treating five candidate selling points as equal, disconnected messages.

## Dimension Pool

### Attraction

- product hero;
- emotional scene;
- color story;
- collection inspiration;
- brand mood.

### Imagination

- fit or overall form;
- dynamic silhouette;
- styling or combination;
- usage scene;
- occasion expression.

### Selling Point And Proof

- design feature;
- material or sensory concept;
- function or mechanism concept;
- craft or process;
- structure close-up;
- multi-angle view;
- human-worn or product-only close-up;
- comparison, component, or kit.

### Decision Support

- size, specification, material, ingredient, color, or SKU;
- usage, care, package contents, or caution;
- brand, review, certification, test, award, or trust information.

## Selection Heuristic

Choose pages that collectively deliver:

- attraction;
- use or wearing imagination;
- product proof;
- decision support when information exists;
- distinct visual carriers and page ideas.

Also verify the six mandatory roles: product recognition, core value, advantage expression, proof, usage imagination, and purchase decision. Missing any role makes the plan incomplete regardless of image count.

Do not expose numerical scoring to the user and do not send it to image generation. Prefer a stable sequence with one or two justified substitutions over rebuilding every set from scratch.

## Five Candidate Selling Points

Always propose five candidates with source status and a suggested headline. Creative mood and conceptual metaphor may be proposed freely; objective commercial facts require support. Ask the user only when the selected sequence depends on an unsupported objective claim.

## Replacement Logic

When a planned module lacks information:

- replace unsupported facts with visible product proof;
- replace an unavailable review with comparison, detail, or another trust carrier;
- replace missing parameters with confirmed information at a glance;
- replace technical performance with a clearly conceptual aesthetic or sensory page;
- never fill a slot with generic mood imagery that adds no product meaning.

# Visual Style Packs

Use the seven packs below as the complete style library. Always display all seven packs in the consolidated plan with a concise product-specific fit rating and one-line effect description. Mark one recommended pack, one safe alternative, and optionally one high-contrast alternative.

Visibility does not mean seven mandatory decisions. The user only needs to confirm, replace, or combine the recommended direction.

Each proposed direction must include buyer fit, commercial mood, background system, palette, typography, layout axis, preferred page archetypes, copy tone, and tradeoff.

## 1. Magazine Editorial

- Best for: fashion, accessories, fragrance, premium lifestyle products.
- Mood: polished, aspirational, controlled, image-led.
- Background: cinematic interiors, studio architecture, paper/editorial systems.
- Typography: high-contrast serif headlines with restrained sans-serif support.
- Layout axis: horizontal by default; one short vertical-title page allowed.
- Preferred archetypes: cinematic hero, single-feature close-up, color moodboard, asymmetric styling scene.
- Copy tone: concise, memorable, elegant.

## 2. Watercolor Art

- Best for: soft lifestyle, handmade, snacks, pet, mother-and-baby, gentle gift products.
- Mood: warm, soft, hand-crafted.
- Background: watercolor washes, paper texture, hand-painted accents.
- Typography: gentle serif, rounded sans-serif, or controlled handwriting.
- Layout axis: horizontal.
- Preferred archetypes: illustrated hero, ingredient/component story, moodboard, usage guide.
- Copy tone: intimate, warm, sensory.

## 3. Tech Future

- Best for: electronics, tools, lighting, smart devices, sports equipment.
- Mood: precise, advanced, professional.
- Background: cool geometric space, controlled glow, technical grids.
- Typography: geometric sans-serif with strong hierarchy.
- Layout axis: horizontal and grid-based.
- Preferred archetypes: technology concept, feature visualization, specification file, comparison split.
- Copy tone: direct, capability-led, data-aware.

## 4. Retro Film

- Best for: coffee, food, fragrance, fashion, nostalgic lifestyle products.
- Mood: atmospheric, tactile, nostalgic.
- Background: warm interiors, film grain, paper, muted natural scenes.
- Typography: editorial serif with small caption text.
- Layout axis: horizontal.
- Preferred archetypes: cinematic hero, lifestyle scene, story page, color inspiration.
- Copy tone: narrative, evocative, human.

## 5. Nordic Minimal

- Best for: apparel basics, home goods, skincare, stationery, simple premium products.
- Mood: calm, clean, rational.
- Background: seamless studio, pale natural materials, geometric whitespace.
- Typography: thin sans-serif or restrained serif.
- Layout axis: horizontal with strict alignment.
- Preferred archetypes: product hero, structure close-up, specification file, comparison split.
- Copy tone: plain, precise, low-hype.

## 6. Neon Cyber

- Best for: gaming, bold electronics, nightlife, youth-oriented futuristic products.
- Mood: energetic, urban, high-contrast.
- Background: dark city abstractions, neon outlines, controlled reflections.
- Typography: heavy sans-serif, outline effects, compact data labels.
- Layout axis: horizontal or modular grid.
- Preferred archetypes: campaign hero, function concept, comparison, parameter dashboard.
- Copy tone: short, forceful, energetic.

## 7. Natural Organic

- Best for: food, wellness, outdoor, pet, sustainable and plant-linked products.
- Mood: grounded, natural, trustworthy.
- Background: earth-tone materials, natural light, botanical or outdoor systems.
- Typography: humanist serif or soft sans-serif.
- Layout axis: horizontal.
- Preferred archetypes: natural hero, origin story, component detail, usage guide.
- Copy tone: concrete, calm, origin- and process-oriented.

## Selection Rules

- Recommend from product category, source product identity, target buyer, price position, and platform.
- Show every pack in a compact table with: style name, fit rating, likely visual effect, and any important tradeoff.
- Use a consistent rating scale such as 1-5 stars or `low / medium / high`; ratings are guidance, not factual product scores.
- Make clear when a tailored phrase such as `fresh magazine editorial` is an application of an existing pack rather than a new eighth style.
- Style affects presentation, not factual authority.
- Keep style pack selection separate from information-use mode.
- Lock one primary pack for the set. Borrow at most one secondary motif without mixing incompatible systems.
